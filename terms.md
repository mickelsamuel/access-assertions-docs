## What the app does

The app tests access rules you define against the effective permissions in your Jira Cloud site and reports what it can prove: PASS when every required source was read and no violation exists, FAIL when a violation was positively proven, UNKNOWN when the proof could not be completed. It is a reporting tool. It does not modify permissions, and its results are statements about what the app could observe at the time of each run — not a certification of your site.
